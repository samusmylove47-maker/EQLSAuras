# The raid lockouts and the weekly log archive — 29 August

**Nothing here is a request or a condition.** It is your app and your call. This is what was built,
what it does to your files, what has been tested, and the one thing that has never happened yet.

---

## What it is

Two things, on one branch.

**A Lockouts page.** It reads your logs and shows a grid of raid bosses against difficulty tiers,
saying which you have killed this lockout week. It uses a parser written by another session and
vendored in unchanged.

**A weekly log archive.** Every Tuesday after the 11:00 raid reset, =Auras copies your live log into
`Logs\Archive\` and empties the original — the same thing the **Archive log now** button already
does, once a week, automatically. That is your own description of it, from the 25th.

The archive is *why* the Lockouts page can be trusted. If the live log is emptied at the reset, then
everything left in it belongs to this week **because there is nothing else in the file**. Nothing
has to be inferred.

Every rotation tested has been in a temporary folder. On this machine the feature has always
correctly *refused*, because your live log contains play from both before and after the last reset,

test is for, and it is the honest reason to say "test this" rather than "this is done".

## If you want to test it deliberately rather than wait for Tuesday

**Press "Archive log now" while EverQuest is running.** That is the same copy-and-empty the weekly
archive performs, on your command, and it is already in the app.

Doing that once would also settle something I could not measure. When a file is emptied underneath a
program that has it open, one of two things happens: the program carries on writing normally, or it
carries on writing at its *old position* and leaves the front of the file full of blank padding.
Which one EverQuest does is **unknown** — there is no `Logs\Archive` folder on this machine, so that
button has never been pressed here, and no log has ever been emptied to find out.

After pressing it, open the fresh log. If the first line is a normal timestamped line, EverQuest
appends and all is well. If it starts with a long run of blank characters, it keeps its position.
**The code now survives either answer**, but knowing which would be worth having.

## What to expect, so it does not look broken when it is working

**The Setup page, under Archive log.** There is a new switch and a line beneath it that says what
the archive is doing. Most of the time it will read something like *"Waiting for a quiet moment —
the game is writing to the log right now."* That is it working. It waits for a lull rather than
emptying a file mid-fight, which is your own warning on that card.

**After the first real archive, the Lockouts page will go blank.** Every cell will read *not
looked*, and it will stay that way until you play. **That is correct** — the week has just started
and nothing has been observed in it yet. It will fill in as you play. It is the single most likely
thing to look like a bug when it is not.

**The page will tell you what it does not know.** Right now, on your logs, it says: *"Your logs have
9 gaps in this period, 68.0h in total"*. That is deliberate. A boss cell can read *open* while
sitting on top of hours you did not have logging on for, and the page says so rather than hiding it.

**One cell reads "depends on the reset hour".** The reset hour you measured on the 29th — Tuesday
11:00 — is used by the archive, but the lockout parser has its own rule that was never given that
number, and it still says so. The Setup card now explains that disagreement rather than leaving you
to find it.

## What it will not do

- **Nothing is deleted.** Every week is copied into `Logs\Archive\` and the copy is checked before
  the original is emptied. If anything at all goes wrong, the log is left exactly as it was.
- **It will not touch your auras.** Those live somewhere else entirely, and there is a test that
  fails if this code ever learns how to reach them.
- **It will not archive a log you have already played on since the reset.** It refuses, and says so.
- **Archived weeks stay on disk but the Lockouts page will not read them.** "Nothing is deleted" is
  true of the file and not of what the tool can see.

## Three things worth deciding

**1. The weekly archive now starts switched OFF, and I changed that late.**

You described it as automatic and it was on by default. A review found a real defect the same
afternoon — see below — and on the strength of that I turned the default off. This is the one part
of =Auras that changes your game files on a timer, it has never once run on a real machine, and it
should not arm itself before you have watched it work. **The switch is on the Setup page and one
line changes the default back** once you are happy with it. Your choice sticks either way.

**The defect, because it is the reason.** The raid reset is Tuesday 11:00 and the archive cut there.
But the Lockouts page counts a period from **Tuesday midnight**, on purpose — the parser was never
given the hour and refuses to guess one. So the archive was removing eleven hours the page still
counts, and to that page a missing eleven hours is not "archived", it is a gap. A gap under a day is
treated as unimportant, so the cells stayed reading **open**.

Which means: a boss killed at eight on a Tuesday morning would have been archived away, and the page
would then have told you the raid was available. That is being confidently wrong in the worst
direction — sending you to re-clear something you are already locked out of.

**It never happened to you.** I checked against your real log rather than a made-up one: simulating
the archive changed no cell at all — 10 done, 14 open, 1 uncertain, before and after. That is only
because you have not raided between midnight and 11:00 in the weeks recorded. A Monday night that
runs past midnight would have done it.

It is fixed: the cut now happens at midnight so the page keeps everything it counts, and anything in
that ambiguous morning is left for the page to mark *uncertain*, which is the honest answer.

**2. Nothing clears out `Logs\Archive\`.** At your rate that is roughly 94 MB a week — about 1.7 GB
after eighteen weeks — and `Logs\Split\` already holds the same content again. Nothing warns you.

**3. There is a bug in the app as it stands today, and it is not new.** If `splitProgress.json` is
ever lost, corrupted, or half-written by a crash, the splitter starts again from the beginning and
**every line ends up in the per-day files twice**. It behaves identically on `master`, so it is not
something this branch introduced, and ten days of real use have not hit it. It is worth knowing
about and worth fixing at some point.
