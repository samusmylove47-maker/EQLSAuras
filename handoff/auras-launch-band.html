EQLS Auras

Stop alt-tabbing to check your buffs. EQLS Auras reads your EverQuest Legends log and shows a countdown overlay on top of the game.

Add overlays and put them where you want. Point an aura at any line in your log, not just buffs the app knows. It shows what you have landed on each groupmate, skins your action bar, routes you across the map, and tracks the weekly raid lockouts EQ never prints. One key hides the lot.

It reads files the client already has: the log it writes as you play, your spellbook, and the game’s own spell icons. It does not read or alter the game’s memory, inject code into it, or send it input. No telemetry, no analytics, no update check.
